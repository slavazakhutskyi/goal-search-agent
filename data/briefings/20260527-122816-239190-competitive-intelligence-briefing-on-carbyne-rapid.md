## 1. 🔵 CARBYNE
**Parent: Axon Enterprise (Nasdaq: AXON)**
**Product: APEX — Cloud-native NG911 call-handling platform**

### 💰 Funding
- **$100M Series D** raised July 31, 2025 — investors included **AT&T Ventures, Axon Enterprise, Cox Enterprises, Global Medical Response, Hanaco Growth, Hercules Capital, RCM/Rokos Capital, and SVB/First Citizens Bank.** Goldman Sachs advised.
- **Acquisition by Axon at $625M** (announced Nov 4, 2025; expected close Q1 2026 → now closed, branded as **"Axon 911"**).

### 📦 Product Launches
- **APEX Continuity** (launched May 19, 2025 with AT&T): Cloud-based emergency backup/failover solution for PSAPs on AT&T ESInet™. Enables 911/Text-to-911 continuity during outages, cyberattacks, system failures, or evacuations. Deployable on laptops, CAD PCs, and backup facilities.
- **APEX AI Suite**: Fully embedded in APEX platform — real-time AI translation (multilingual), automated dispatch protocol assistance, live video integration (patented), unified voice/video/chat/location interface.
- **Axon 911**: Post-acquisition brand combining Carbyne (cloud call routing/handling) + Prepared (AI situational intelligence) into a single integrated 911 platform — "call to courtroom."

### 🤝 Partnerships
- **AT&T**: Joint launch of APEX Continuity; AT&T ESInet distribution; AT&T Ventures invested in $100M round.
- **Global Medical Response (GMR)**: Strategic investor + operational partner.
- **Axon Ecosystem**: Integration with Axon Evidence (digital evidence management, 500K+ users), body cams, TASER, dashcam, and Axon Records.

### 🏆 Customer Wins / Recognition
- **477% YoY growth in APEX ARR** (as of July 2025); **+105% growth in APEX platform customers**.
- **~300 sites worldwide**, across **23 U.S. states and 6 countries**; **7,000+ call-takers** using Carbyne globally.
- **Frost & Sullivan 2025 North America Product Leadership Award** (Oct 14, 2025) for Excellence in Emergency Cloud Call Handling.
- Serving agencies protecting **250M+ people worldwide**.

### 🔺 RapidSOS Implications
Carbyne/Axon is now the most formidable platform threat — they own the call-handling layer (Carbyne), AI layer (Prepared), devices (TASER, cameras), and evidence (Axon Evidence). The "call to courtroom" narrative directly competes with RapidSOS's data intelligence positioning. Watch for Axon 911 bundling to displace standalone RapidSOS data integrations at PSAPs.

## 2. 🟠 RAPIDDEPLOY
**Parent: Motorola Solutions (NYSE: MSI)**
**Products: Radius Mapping, Lightning (mobile), Eclipse Analytics**

### 💰 Funding / Acquisition
- **Acquired by Motorola Solutions** on **February 23, 2025** (terms undisclosed). Motorola plans to integrate RapidDeploy's NG911 suite into **VESTA 911 call-handling** and the **AI-powered VESTA NXT** platform.

### 📦 Product Launches / Integrations
- **VESTA NXT Integration** (in progress): RapidDeploy's Radius Mapping, Lightning, and Eclipse Analytics are being integrated into Motorola's VESTA ecosystem, enabling radio-to-PSAP emergency button alerts (responder GPS location auto-surfaced in Radius and Lightning simultaneously).
- **ECC Interagency Messaging**: Real-time secure cross-jurisdictional chat between ECCs and first responders (demonstrated in active shooter scenarios).
- **Critical Incident Board (CIB)**: Aggregates CCTV, RapidVideo, and third-party feeds into a centralized visual hub in Radius Mapping and Lightning.
- **Breadcrumb Location Tracking**: Real-time caller movement tracking.
- **ZeroEyes Integration** (Nov 2024): AI gun detection from ZeroEyes layers into Radius Mapping — real-time suspect location, human-validated gun images, and panic proximity alerts pushed to Lightning app. Focused on K-12 school safety.
- **Raptor Technologies Integration**: Panic button integration for schools.

### 🤝 Partnerships
- **ZeroEyes** (AI gun detection, DHS SAFETY Act Designated) — K-12 school safety focus.
- **Motorola Solutions full ecosystem**: VESTA 911, PremierOne CAD, Rave Suite, APX radios, body cameras.
- **GeoComm, Datamark, NextNav, CRG**: Indoor floor plan integrations.
- **Singlewire, CrisisGo**: Panic button integrations.

### 🏆 Customer Wins
- **Q1–Q2 2025 NG911 expansion**: New contracts in **Florida (23 counties + 5 additional agencies)**, **Morgan County, Indiana**, **McHenry County, Illinois**, **Adams County, Colorado**, **Stark County, Ohio**.
- **Alabama 911 Board** statewide contract (2024).
- **Kansas 911** adopted full NG911 suite (Radius Mapping, Lightning, Eclipse Analytics).
- **Minnesota State Patrol** — Superstar Award for cross-border life-saving rescue using Radius Mapping (Feb 2025).
- Now serves **1,600+ PSAPs/ECCs across 24 states**.
- **GovTech 100** for 8th consecutive year (2025).

### 🔺 RapidSOS Implications
Motorola's acquisition gives RapidDeploy distribution at scale through Motorola's massive existing PSAP installed base (VESTA is the dominant CAD/call-handling platform). VESTA NXT integration could make RapidDeploy the default NG911 data layer for Motorola customers — potentially displacing or commoditizing RapidSOS data feeds in those agencies. The ZeroEyes school safety angle is a differentiated vertical worth monitoring.
