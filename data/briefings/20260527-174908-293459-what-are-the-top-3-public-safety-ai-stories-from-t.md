## Key themes
- **Dispatch automation addressing critical staffing gaps:** Dispatcher vacancy rates nationally now exceed 30% (up from 15–20% pre-COVID), pushing agencies toward AI chatbots for nonemergency intake and call triage.[^1]
- **Role-based AI agents optimizing workflows:** Emerging products tailor intelligence to dispatch centers and field officers—real-time transcription, keyword flagging ("gun," "heart attack"), and voice-command data retrieval inform responders en route.[^2]
- **Predictive analytics enabling proactive care models:** EMS and fire departments are using AI-driven syndromic surveillance to spot public health trends early and shift toward community paramedicine, especially in rural underserved areas.[^3]
- **Balancing automation with human oversight:** Hybrid models preserve critical decision-making and discretion; emotion-detection AI shows only 77% accuracy, raising stakes for emergency vs. nonemergency triage.[^1]
- **Transparency and governance as adoption prerequisites:** Agencies are partnering with legislators, POST commissions, and IT staff to establish cybersecurity, bias audits, and privacy safeguards before rolling out autonomous systems.[^1]
