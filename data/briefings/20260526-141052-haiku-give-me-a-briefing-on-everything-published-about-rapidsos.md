## TL;DR
RapidSOS announced a major integration with AT&T's ESInet infrastructure on April 29, making HARMONY AI available directly through the nation's most widely deployed 911 network without requiring agencies to expose systems to the public internet. The move removes a years-long barrier for security-conscious Emergency Communication Centers and is being piloted at scale in North Carolina following Hurricane Helene. Actor Jeremy Renner joined as partner and investor, citing his own 2023 rescue as motivation to support first responders with better real-time intelligence tools. [^1][^2][^4]
